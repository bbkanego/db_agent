<database_schema>
You have access to database with tables:
Schema: employees, Table: employee, columns: (id, first_name, last_name, birth_date, gender, hire_date)
Details of "employee" table: In employee table "id" is the primary key and can be used for joins with
tables "department_employee", "department_manager", "salary" and "title" using their "employee_id" columns.
Gender column has values F for Female and M for Male.

Schema: employees, Table: department, columns: (id, dept_name)
Details of "department" table: In department table "id" is the primary key and id of the department and can be used for joins
with other tables. The "dept_name" is the name of the department.

Schema: employees, Table: department_employee, columns: (employee_id, department_id, from_date, to_date)
Details of "department_employee" table: In department_employee table, "employee_id" and "department_id"
together form a "composite" primary key.The "from_date" indicates when the employee started in the department.
Use to_date > CURRENT_DATE for current records.

Schema: employees, Table: department_manager, columns: (employee_id, department_id, from_date, to_date)
Details of "department_manager" table: In department_manager table, "employee_id" and "department_id"
together form a "composite" primary key. The "from_date" indicates when the employee became the manager of the department.
Use to_date > CURRENT_DATE for current records.

Schema: employees, Table: salary, columns: (employee_id, amount, from_date, to_date)
Details of "salary" table: In salary table, "employee_id" and "from_date" together form a "composite"
primary key. The "from_date" indicates when the employee started getting the salary.
Use to_date > CURRENT_DATE for current records.

Schema: employees, Table: title, columns: (employee_id, title, from_date, to_date)
Details of "title" table: In title table, "employee_id", "title" and "from_date" together form a
"composite" primary key. The "from_date" indicates when the employee got the title/position.
Use to_date > CURRENT_DATE for current records.
</database_schema>

2. What is the first name, last name, gender, date of hire, birth date and employee id of the past manager of Marketing department?
    Sample query: "SELECT
                                e.first_name,
                                e.last_name,
                                e.gender,
                                TO_CHAR(e.hire_date, 'YYYY-MM-DD'),
                                TO_CHAR(dm.to_date, 'YYYY-MM-DD') AS end_of_employment,
                                TO_CHAR(e.birth_date, 'YYYY-MM-DD'),
                                e.id AS employee_id

                              JOIN employees.department_manager AS dm
                                ON e.id = dm.employee_id
                              JOIN employees.department AS d
                                ON dm.department_id = d.id
