1. Who are the employees that are working in the Marketing department who make more
than 100K and can be called as baby boomers?
SELECT e.first_name, e.last_name, e.gender, e.hire_date, e.birth_date AS birthdate, s.amount AS salary, d.dept_name AS department
FROM employees.employee AS e
JOIN employees.salary AS s ON e.id = s.employee_id
JOIN employees.department_employee AS de ON e.id = de.employee_id
JOIN employees.department AS d ON de.department_id = d.id
WHERE d.dept_name = 'Marketing' AND e.birth_date BETWEEN '1946-01-01' AND '1964-12-31'
AND s.amount > 100000 AND s.to_date > CURRENT_DATE AND de.to_date > CURRENT_DATE

4. Who are the employees that are working in the Marketing department who make between 105K and 110K
and can be called as baby boomers?

SELECT e.first_name, e.last_name, e.gender, e.hire_date, e.birth_date AS birthdate,
s.amount AS salary, d.dept_name AS department FROM employees.employee
AS e JOIN employees.salary AS s ON e.id = s.employee_id
JOIN employees.department_employee AS de ON e.id = de.employee_id
JOIN employees.department AS d ON de.department_id = d.id
WHERE d.dept_name = 'Marketing' AND e.birth_date
BETWEEN '1946-01-01' AND '1964-12-31' AND
s.amount BETWEEN 105000 AND 110000 AND s.to_date > CURRENT_DATE AND de.to_date > CURRENT_DATE;

5. Who are the Manager employees that are working in the Marketing department who
make between 105K and 110K and can be called as baby boomers?

SELECT e.first_name, e.last_name, e.gender, e.hire_date, e.birth_date AS birthdate, s.amount AS salary,
d.dept_name AS department FROM employees.employee AS e
JOIN employees.department_manager AS dm ON e.id = dm.employee_id
JOIN employees.salary AS s ON e.id = s.employee_id
JOIN employees.department_employee AS de ON e.id = de.employee_id
JOIN employees.department AS d ON de.department_id = d.id
WHERE d.dept_name = 'Marketing' AND e.birth_date BETWEEN '1946-01-01' AND '1964-12-31'
AND s.amount BETWEEN 105000 AND 110000 AND dm.to_date > CURRENT_DATE AND de.to_date > CURRENT_DATE

7. What are the titles of various people working in Marketing department?
